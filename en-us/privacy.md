# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 4, 2026

This policy explains how personal data is handled in the apps published by **Alexandre B.** ("I", "me"), an independent developer based in Brazil. It applies to every app listed at [alexandreb.dev](/en/). General rules are in sections 1–10; each app's specifics are in section 11.

I process personal data in accordance with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Lei nº 13.709/2018, "LGPD").

## 1. Controller and contact

The data controller is Alexandre B. For any privacy matter, including exercising your rights, email [contato@alexandreb.dev](mailto:contato@alexandreb.dev).

## 2. Data I collect

Depending on the app, I may collect:

- **Account data:** name, email address and profile picture, when you sign in (including through third-party sign-in such as Google).
- **Content you provide:** data you upload, type or import into an app. See section 11 for each app.
- **Technical data:** IP address, browser type, device information, request logs and error reports, used to operate and secure the service.
- **Cookies:** only those needed for sign-in sessions and basic functioning. I do not use advertising cookies.

I do not sell personal data, and I do not use it for advertising.

## 7. Retention

I keep personal data while your account is active or as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for. After you delete your account or request deletion, data is deleted within 30 days, except where the law requires me to keep it. Technical logs are kept for up to 90 days.

## 8. Security

I use encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls and providers with recognized security practices. No system is completely secure, but I take reasonable measures to protect your data.

## 9. Your rights

Under LGPD art. 18, you can ask to: confirm whether I process your data; access it; correct it; anonymize, block or delete unnecessary data; port it; delete data processed based on consent; learn who I share it with; and withdraw consent.

To exercise these rights, email [contato@alexandreb.dev](mailto:contato@alexandreb.dev). I will respond within 15 days. You can also file a complaint with Brazil's data protection authority (ANPD).

To delete your data, see [Data Deletion](/en/data-deletion).

## 10. Children

The apps are not intended for people under 18, and I do not knowingly collect their data.

## 11. App-specific information

### brnf {#brnf}

brnf helps organize and query Brazilian electronic invoice (*nota fiscal*) data.

- **What it processes:** invoice data you provide, which may include issuer and recipient names, CNPJ or CPF numbers, addresses, invoice amounts, items and dates. When an invoice involves an individual, this is personal data.
- **Why:** to store, validate and display your invoice records.
- **Where it is stored:** Airtable.
- **Your responsibility:** you should only upload invoices you have a legitimate right to process (for example, invoices you issued or received).
