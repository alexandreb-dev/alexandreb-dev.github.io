# Data Deletion

You can ask me to delete your account and personal data from any of my apps at any time.

## How to request

1. Send an email to [contato@alexandreb.dev](mailto:contato@alexandreb.dev) with the subject **"Data deletion request"**.
2. Include the app name and the email address linked to your account.
3. I may ask you to confirm the request from that email address, to make sure it's you.

## What happens next

- I confirm receipt within 15 days.
- Your account and personal data are deleted within 30 days.
- Data I must keep by law, if any, is retained only for that purpose and then deleted.
- Backups are overwritten in their normal cycle.

## Sign-in with Facebook or Google

If you signed in with Facebook or Google, you can also remove the app's access from that account's settings. Removing access stops new data from being shared, but to delete data already stored, send the request above.
